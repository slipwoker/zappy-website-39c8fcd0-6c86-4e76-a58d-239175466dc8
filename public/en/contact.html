Frequently Asked Questions

We've compiled answers to the questions we receive most frequently. Didn't find what you were looking for? Don't hesitate to contact us directly.

How do we start the purchasing process?

The process is simple and convenient. Contact us by phone, WhatsApp, or through the contact form on the website. We'll hear what you're looking for — vehicle type, budget, daily use — and from there we start working for you. At a car dealership like ours, every customer receives personal guidance from the very first moment.

What details should I prepare in advance?

The more we know, the better we can match you with the perfect vehicle. We recommend thinking ahead about your planned budget, number of regular passengers, main type of driving (city, highways, off-road), and if you have preferences regarding a specific manufacturer, model, or transmission. Even if you don't have answers to all the questions — that's perfectly fine, that's what we're here for.

How long does it take to get a response?

We commit to getting back to you within one business day at most. In most cases, the response comes much faster — usually within a few hours. If you contacted us outside business hours, we'll get back to you immediately at the start of the next business day.

Can I see the vehicle before purchasing?

Absolutely. We believe a car should feel right, not just look good in pictures. We'll schedule a convenient time for you to view the vehicle, conduct a comprehensive inspection, and even take a test drive — so you can make a decision with complete confidence. Everything at your pace, without pressure.

How do your recommendations work?

We don't offer 'what's in stock' — we listen, ask questions, and understand your real needs. Based on our experience with hundreds of customers, we recommend vehicles that suit your lifestyle, budget, and priorities. Whether it's eco-friendly electric vehicles or spacious family cars — the recommendation is always personally tailored.
